Truck sizes for house shifting in India

Short answer: household moves in India typically use one of seven vehicle classes: a mini goods vehicle, an 8 ft pickup, a 10 ft, 14 ft, 17 ft, 19 ft or 22 ft closed truck. The sizes below are typical cargo bodies for each class, not specifications of a particular vehicle. Last reviewed November 2026.

How to read these numbers

Internal cargo dimensions
The inside length, width and height of the load body. Tall or wide items can fail on one dimension even when total volume is fine.
Gross volume
Length × width × height, in cubic feet.
Usable volume
Gross volume × 0.75, because real loads leave gaps.
Payload
How much weight the vehicle may carry. It is a different limit from volume. It is not stated here because no verified payload is held for these classes: ask the transport provider.

Where the sizes come from

The cargo sizes are typical figures quoted by Indian transport and rental operators for each class, rounded for planning. They vary between operators, makes and body builders, which is why they are labelled class-typical and unverified.

To see how much published figures vary, take the Tata Ace Gold mini truck: Tata Motors lists its cargo box at about 2.2 to 2.5 m long (depending on the variant), 1.49 m wide and 0.3 m high, an open deck rather than a covered body. See Tata Motors' Ace Gold CNG specifications. A covered body adds height, so the mini goods vehicle class here is not an Ace specification.

Before relying on any estimate, confirm the real internal dimensions and payload with your transport provider. When verified figures for specific vehicles are available they can replace these class-typical values in one data file.
